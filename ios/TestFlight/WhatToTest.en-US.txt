WHAT'S NEW

Menus that feel better
• Menus spring out of the button that opened them, and touching outside one closes it and scrolls the page in the same swipe
• Slide your finger over a menu and let go on what you want, with a tick as you pass each item

A fresh look for Boards, Bookmarks and Saved
• On the Boards tab, Continue reading shows the threads you were last in as big pictures, with how many replies are new; it links to History, and can be turned off in Settings → History
• In the Bookmarks tab, threads with new replies come first, as big pictures
• The Saved tab groups your files by when you saved them, under Today, Yesterday and so on
• With no bookmarks yet, the Bookmarks tab says how to add them, with a button to the boards
• A board, your feed and a board's archive start straight with their threads, with how many there are under the title

The same page slide on Android
• Pages slide in and out on Android as they do on iPhone, with the page underneath moving along
