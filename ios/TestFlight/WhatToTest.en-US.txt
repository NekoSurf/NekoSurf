WHAT'S NEW

Made for iPad and tablets
• In a wide window, the tabs move down the side, and a thread opens beside the board, bookmarks, history or search you picked it from
• Lists and threads keep to a column you can read, and a tall image fits on the screen instead of running past it
• With a trackpad or mouse, right-click for the menu that holding opens
• With a keyboard, the arrows or J and K go post by post, Cmd+R refreshes, Cmd+F searches a board, Cmd+1 to Cmd+4 switch tabs, and Esc goes back

Catch up on a thread
• While a thread has posts you haven't read, a button at the bottom says how many and takes you to the next one, unfolding replies it's folded away in; with a keyboard, N does the same
• Mark All as Read, in a thread's menu, clears its unread count, so a long thread you only skimmed stops showing replies to catch up on in Bookmarks and History

Threads that still open offline
• Threads you bookmarked or visited open without a connection, or once 4chan has deleted them, as they last loaded, with the time of that copy at the top

Save threads for offline
• Save for Offline, in a thread's menu, downloads every image and video in it, so you can read it later without a connection; the line under the title shows how far along it is
• Saved threads are listed in Bookmarks → More → Saved for Offline and in Settings → Storage, with how much space each takes, to open or remove
• Saving a thread again adds what's new since, and tries what couldn't be downloaded

WHAT'S FIXED

• Sharing works on iPad: the share sheet opens next to the share button, for threads, images and videos, Saved files, backups and the iCloud sync log
