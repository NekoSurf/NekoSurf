SWIPE BACK FROM ANYWHERE
On a board, a thread, History, search or Settings, swipe right starting from the middle of the screen.

• The page should follow your finger with the previous page showing behind it, and go back once you let go past halfway or flick it.
• Let go while moving: the page should carry on smoothly at your finger's speed, with no jump or stall.
• Let go early after a slow drag: the page should slide back into place.
• Start the swipe on comment text, an image and a board or History row: all should go back, without opening or selecting anything.
• Rows with actions: swipe left to reveal them, then right to close them. That shouldn't go back. Swipe right on a closed row: that should.
• Drag across a video in a thread: it should scrub, not go back. The left edge of the screen should still go back.
• Scroll up and down at a slight angle, as thumbs do: lists should scroll, and it shouldn't go back by accident.

Please also try what couldn't be checked on a simulator:
• How it feels on a real phone: does it ever go back when you only meant to scroll or select text?
• Long-press text to select it, then drag the selection handles: that should still select, not go back.
• In the search field with the keyboard up, try swiping right on the field and on the results.
• The fullscreen viewer and the Saved tab's viewer should work exactly as before, closing only from the left edge.
• On Android, compare it with the system's own back gesture from the edge.

REPLY COUNTS
Find a reply whose text starts with a number, right after the quote (like ">>123" followed by "4 weeks long"). The post it quotes should now count it, and show it on its replies page.
