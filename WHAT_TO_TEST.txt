WHAT'S NEW

Find deleted threads in archives
• On a thread 4chan has deleted, even one opened from a pasted link, tap Look in Archives to read it from an archive that keeps its board, like Desuarchive, 4plebs, warosu or archived.moe, with its images and videos if the archive kept them; it's in the thread's menu too when an old copy is showing
• If an archive's copy falls short, Look in Other Archives in the thread's menu tries the rest, and posts the old copy had stay
• Opened again later, the thread says which archive it came from, and shows the archive's thumbnail in History and Bookmarks
• Turn off any archive you'd rather not have asked, in Settings → Archives

Text size for posts
• Make the text of posts in threads smaller or larger, in Settings → Threads → Text size

WHAT'S FIXED

• Tapping a deleted thread in Bookmarks opens it again, as it last loaded, with Look in Archives if there's no copy; it used to only say the thread was deleted
• The Deleted and Archived labels on thread cards sit by the counts, no longer over a long title
• Threads you visited stay in History once 4chan deletes them, marked Deleted, and still open as they last loaded; opening History used to remove them
• Threads saved for offline open straight away, even on a slow connection, and then update
• New posts' images are saved too while a thread saved for offline is open, and a save cut short, as by closing the app, carries on the next time it's opened
• Saving two threads for offline at once no longer loses the first one's text
• Saving a copy of a deleted thread for offline keeps the date it last loaded
• Saved for Offline and Settings → Storage no longer count threads on hidden boards, and Remove All leaves them; Storage also shows what the copies of threads take up
