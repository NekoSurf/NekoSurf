WHAT'S NEW

Catching up on threads
• Back in a thread in the nested view, replies posted while you were away are at the bottom with the other new posts, each under a preview of the post it answers, and a preview under that post in the thread shows them there in full when tapped; the preview above one at the bottom goes to the post it answers
• A thread's count now includes every post you haven't scrolled to yet, not only those posted since you opened it, and says unread; this starts with threads you open after updating, and only posts that came since your first visit are tinted

Bookmarks that look like a board
• Bookmarked threads show as a board's threads do, in its grid or list, with the board, reply counts and unread posts on each
• Archived and deleted threads say so on their card, and the ••• menu can remove them all at once

A new look for the Saved tab
• Each saved file fills a tile shaped like its picture, so tall and wide ones aren't cut off, with whether it's an image, video or GIF on the picture
• Its name is on a frosted bar, with its file type, size and, for an image, resolution under it
• On iPad, as many columns as fit, as on a board

A tidier tab bar
• The tab bar is only as wide as its tabs, in the middle of the screen
• Tapping a tab gives a little tap and bounce, and tapping the tab you're on goes back to the top of it
• The Bookmarks tab shows how many bookmarked threads have unread posts

WHAT'S FIXED

• With iCloud sync on, a file saved before version 0.13.0 is now in the same place in the Saved tab on all your devices
• In the full-screen viewer, the shade and controls under a video stay at the bottom of the screen while you swipe between videos, instead of moving with each one
• In the full-screen viewer, swiping to the next video no longer makes the one before it flash
• In Bookmarks, a thread opens straight away, without waiting to find out whether it's still up
• Clear bookmarks asks first, and says when it clears them on all your devices
• Bookmarks keeps the order you chose, and ticks it in the Sort menu
• The ring on a saved file going to or from iCloud fills smoothly, instead of starting over with every percent
