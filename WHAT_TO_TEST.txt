WHAT'S NEW

⚙️ All of Settings on one page
• Every setting is now on the Settings tab itself, grouped into Boards, Threads, Media, Hidden threads, History and Storage, with no pages to open
• Choices between two options, like Grid or List, switch right in the row, and each setting explains itself next to its switch
• Clearing watched posts or recently visited threads now asks first

🖼️ Filter a thread's gallery
• In a thread's gallery, show only images or only videos; swiping in the fullscreen viewer stays within what you picked

🔔 New replies on board cards
• Threads you've opened show how many replies are new on their cards in a board and the feed, in both grid and list view

🔢 New look for grid cards
• The thread number now sits on the image in the top left, so cards take less height and more of them fit on screen
• The image fills the whole card, with the thread number, bookmark button and reply counts frosted on top, so they stay readable on bright images

WHAT'S FIXED

• Letting go of a short pull to refresh no longer leaves the ring on screen
• The Saved tab remembers whether you chose newest or oldest first

Please also try what couldn't be checked on a simulator:
• Pull to refresh with a real finger: short pulls let go slowly and quickly, on iPhone and on Android; the ring should always leave, and only a full pull should refresh
• VoiceOver or TalkBack: the Grid/List, Desc/Asc and Nested/Flat choices in Settings, and the new reply count on a grid card, which should read as "2 new"
• Largest text size with Larger Accessibility Sizes on: Settings and grid cards, with a thread number that has to wrap under a Sticky label
• Small phones: list and grid cards with a large reply count and a new reply count at once
• Scrolling a board in grid view on an older phone, especially Android: it should stay smooth with the frosted number, bookmark button and reply counts on every card
