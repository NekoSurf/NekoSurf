WHAT'S NEW

🙈 Spoilers stay covered
• Spoilered images and videos are covered until you tap them, in threads and the viewer; on boards, in search and the gallery they stay covered, and spoilered threads keep no thumbnail in History or Bookmarks

🪪 See who's posting
• Posts show their poster ID, tripcode and staff tag, on the boards that have them

🔄 Refresh a thread yourself
• Pull past the top or bottom of a thread, or choose ⋯ → Refresh, and it tells you how many new posts it found; a ring fills as you pull, you feel a tick when it's full, and a spinner shows until it's done
• Pulling down to refresh now looks and feels the same on the home screen, Feed, boards, Search and History, where the spinner used to be hidden behind the title bar
• The title bar says when a thread is Archived, Closed or Deleted

🔗 Links to other threads and boards
• Quotes of another thread, like "Previous" links, now open and say "(other thread)", and links to other boards open too

💻 Code looks like code
• Code blocks keep their lines and spacing, in a monospaced font, in a box of their own

📶 Data saver
• Turn it on in Settings → Data and threads show only thumbnails: images and videos load when you open them

🔢 Save with 4chan's file names (#91)
• Turn on "Use 4chan's file names" in Settings → Data, and files you save or share are named like 1700000000000.jpg instead of what the poster called them

♿ VoiceOver and TalkBack
• Icon buttons now say what they do

WHAT'S FIXED

• Messages that pop up at the bottom (like "Thread filter added") no longer block the screen: you can keep scrolling and tapping while they show
• Bookmarks no longer say Deleted when 4chan is limiting requests or can't be reached, and can still be opened then
• Tapping a quote of a post that's gone says so, instead of showing an empty post
• Loading stops and offers to try again if the connection goes quiet for 20 seconds, instead of spinning for minutes
• Very large text sizes no longer cut off the title bar, the tabs and cards: text grows to a limit, and the title bar and tabs stay their size
• Backslashes show once, and text someone wrote like &quot; shows as they wrote it
• On Android, deleting a saved file without storage access no longer removes it from the list while leaving the file
• Bookmarking a thread opened from a reply in search keeps its title and image, also with history turned off

Please also try what couldn't be checked on a simulator:
• VoiceOver or TalkBack: the icon buttons in the title bar and the viewer say what they do, like "Gallery" or "Save to Photos"
• Pull to refresh with a real finger: the tick when the ring fills and when you let go, on iPhone and on Android (where lists now bounce at the ends)
• Airplane Mode just after opening a thread or board, or a very slow network: within about 20 seconds it offers to try again
• Bookmarks with no connection stay openable, not Deleted
• Android: code in a monospaced font; deleting a saved file with storage access off shows the permission sheet and keeps the file listed
• Saving a webm to the Saved tab, Photos and sharing it: each ends up as a video that plays (a webm on Android, an mp4 on iPhone); saving several at once, including two files with the same name, overwrites none
• Largest text size with Larger Accessibility Sizes on: nothing cut off on the home screen, a board, a thread and Settings
