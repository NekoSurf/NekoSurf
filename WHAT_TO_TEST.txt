SPOILERS
Find a post with a spoilered image (on /v/ or /vg/, for example).

• It should show a cover with an eye and "Spoiler", in the thread and when swiping to it in the viewer. Tap it: the image or video shows, and stays shown until you close the app.
• On a spoilered thread, or in the gallery and search, the thumbnail stays covered, and tapping goes where it always did.
• Bookmark a thread whose first post is a spoiler: its card should have no image. The same in History.

WHO'S POSTING
On a board with poster IDs, like /qst/ or /pol/: each post's header should show a colored ID:abc123, the same color for the same poster. Tripcodes show after the name, staff tags like ## mod in red.

REFRESHING A THREAD
• Pull well past the bottom or top of a thread: you should feel a tap and see "No new posts" or "3 new posts". Pulling once gives one message, also in a thread shorter than the screen.
• ⋯ → Refresh should do the same.
• Open an archived thread from History: under the title it should say Archived. A closed one says Closed. If a thread is deleted while it's open, it says Deleted the next time it updates.

LINKS
• In a /g/ general, tap "Previous: >>123 (other thread)": that thread opens and its title fills in once loaded.
• Tap a link like >>>/g/: that board opens.
• Tap a quote of a post that was deleted: "Post not found", not an empty post.

CODE
On /g/, find a post with code. It should sit in a shaded box, in a monospaced font, with its indents. Long-press to select it; tapping it folds the post like other text.

DATA SAVER
Settings → Data → Data saver. Open a thread:
• Images show as thumbnails, and stay that way. Videos show a play icon and don't play or download until you tap them.
• Open a video, let it load, and go back: the thread shows its thumbnail and play icon again, not the video.
• In the viewer, the one showing loads. The next one should start when you swipe to it, not before.
Turn it off afterwards: everything should load again.

4CHAN'S FILE NAMES (issue #91)
Settings → Data → Use 4chan's file names. Open an image or video from a thread:
• Share it and choose Save to Files: the name should be a number like 1700000000000.jpg, not the poster's.
• Save it to Photos: in Photos, swipe up on it and the name should be the number too.
• Save it, and open it in the Saved tab: it's listed by the number too, in the viewer as well, and sharing it from there shows the number. Files saved with it on earlier show their number now too.
Turn it off: new saves get the poster's name again, and files already saved keep theirs.

LARGE TEXT
Settings on your phone → Display → Text Size at the largest, with Larger Accessibility Sizes on. The title bar and tabs stay as they are; cards and posts grow a little, with nothing cut off. Check the home screen, a board, a thread, Settings and dialogs.

Please also try what couldn't be checked on a simulator:
• VoiceOver or TalkBack: the icon buttons in the title bar and the viewer should say what they do, like "Gallery" or "Save to Photos".
• Bad connection: turn on Airplane Mode just after opening a thread or board, or use a very slow network. Within about 20 seconds it should offer to try again.
• Bookmarks with no connection: they should stay openable, not say Deleted.
• Android: code blocks should use a monospaced font. Deleting a saved file with storage access off should show the permission sheet and keep the file listed.
• Saving: save a webm to the Saved tab, Save to Photos and share it: each should end up as a video that plays. On Android it stays a webm, on iPhone it becomes an mp4.
• Saving several at once, including two files with the same name: all should appear, none overwritten.
• Bookmarks: open a thread from a link with history turned off and bookmark it: it should keep its title and image.
