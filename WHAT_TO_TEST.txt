WHAT'S NEW

A quicker board list
• The board list on the Boards tab is one line per board, saying what it's about in a few words, grouped by letter
• Swipe a board to add it to your favorites, which aren't listed a second time below them
• Hold a board to add it to your favorites or open its archive
• Hold a thread under Continue reading to bookmark it or take it out of History; the row takes less room when the titles are short

Search in Settings
• Type in the field under the card at the top of Settings to see just the settings that match

Tidier Bookmarks
• Archived and deleted threads move to the end, under No longer live, with a button to remove them all
• Under the title, how many bookmarks you have and how many have new replies

Small touches
• A board's name now fits at the top of its page: the view, the archive and the sort order are in one menu next to the heart
• A thread's title has more room at the top of its page too: the gallery is in its menu, beside the bookmark
• The heart on a board's page is pink, like your favorites on the Boards tab
• Menus are worded the same way everywhere ("Mark all as read", "Scroll to top"), and the Saved tab's page is called Saved
• Empty pages, and pages that fail to load, look the same everywhere and say what to do: a board or your feed with no threads to show, History, Saved for offline, Saved and Bookmarks
• A light tap is felt when you favorite a board, bookmark a thread or save a file, and a tick when you pick from a menu or change a setting

WHAT'S FIXED

• Tapping outside a menu only closes it, instead of also tapping what's underneath; a swipe still scrolls the page
