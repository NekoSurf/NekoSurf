## What's new

### 🔍 Search
- New **search button** on the home screen
- **Favorite boards**: search the threads on all your favorite boards at once, updating as you type
- **All of 4chan**: search every post on 4chan through 4chan's own search (find.4chan.org)
  - Results show the board, a thumbnail and whether it's a thread or a reply
  - Tap a reply to jump straight to it in its thread; it's briefly highlighted so you can spot it
  - Now and then 4chan asks to confirm you're human first; just complete the check when it appears
  - If the search can't load, open it in your browser with one tap
- Searching now finds posts with **all your words in any order**, also in board and archive search

### 🕘 History
- **Continue** on the home screen is now a full **History page**, opened with the clock button
- Your last 100 threads, grouped by day, with new-reply counts and archived status
- Swipe to remove a thread, or clear it all at once

### ✨ Improvements
- Jumping to a post (resuming, "new posts", closing an image) no longer hides it under the header
- Threads that were deleted now say so, with a button to open them on 4chan
- A cleaner screen when something fails to load, with a clear "Try again" button
- Changing the sort in your feed is instant instead of reloading everything
