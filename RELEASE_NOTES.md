## What's new

### 🎄 Holiday themes
- On Christmas, Halloween, New Year's, Valentine's Day, St. Patrick's Day and Easter, the app wears the holiday's colors, and snow, leaves, confetti, hearts, clovers or petals drift down the screen
- Your own color comes back after the holiday; OLED stays black
- Turn the colors and the falling snow off on their own, under Settings → Theme
- Pick a holiday under Settings → Theme → Holiday to keep it all year, like Christmas in July

### 📁 Choose where Save to Photos goes, on Android
- Pick any folder for it, an SD card's too, under Settings → Media → Download folder
- Turn on A folder per thread to keep each thread's files together, in a folder named by its number and subject, like "123456 - Subject"
