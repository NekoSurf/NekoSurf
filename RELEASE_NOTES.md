## What's new

### 🙈 Spoilers stay covered
- Spoilered images and videos are covered until you tap them, in threads and the viewer; on boards, in search and the gallery they stay covered, and spoilered threads keep no thumbnail in History or Bookmarks

### 🪪 See who's posting
- Posts show their poster ID, tripcode and staff tag, on the boards that have them

### 🔄 Refresh a thread yourself
- Pull past the top or bottom of a thread, or choose ⋯ → Refresh, and it tells you how many new posts it found
- The title bar says when a thread is Archived, Closed or Deleted

### 🔗 Links to other threads and boards
- Quotes of another thread, like "Previous" links, now open and say "(other thread)", and links to other boards open too

## What's fixed

- Bookmarks no longer say Deleted when 4chan is limiting requests or can't be reached, and can still be opened then
- Tapping a quote of a post that's gone says so, instead of showing an empty post
- Loading stops and offers to try again if the connection goes quiet for 20 seconds, instead of spinning for minutes
- Very large text sizes no longer cut off the title bar, the tabs and cards: text grows to a limit, and the title bar and tabs stay their size
- Backslashes show once, and text someone wrote like &quot; shows as they wrote it
- On Android, deleting a saved file without storage access no longer removes it from the list while leaving the file
