## What's new

### 📌 New replies stay at the bottom until you've read them
- Replies that came in since your last visit stay at the bottom of the thread, under a preview of the post they answer, until you've read them all and newer ones have come
- Replies that come in while a thread is open are listed at the bottom the same way

### 🧹 Edit filters, and check what thread filters catch
- Tap a thread filter or a keyword post filter in Settings to edit it, e.g. to cut one you added by holding a thread down to the words that matter
- Turn off Hide matching threads in Settings to see the threads your filters catch, faded, in boards, the feed and search

### 📱 Ready for iPhone Duo
- Open, a thread you pick from a board, Bookmarks, History or Search opens beside the list, as on iPad; fold it shut and the thread stays open, and goes back beside the list when you open it again
- On both screens, the camera and status icons no longer cover buttons or text
- On an 11-inch iPad held upright, threads you pick from a board open beside it too, with the board in a narrower column

## What's fixed

- Reopening a thread after reading new replies now brings you back to where you stopped, also when they came in while the thread was open, not up near the opening post
