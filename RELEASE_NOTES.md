## What's new

### ☁️ iCloud sync
- In the Saved tab, a saved file that isn't in iCloud yet shows a cloud while it waits its turn, then a ring filling as it goes up
- In Settings, iCloud sync › Sync log shows what sync did lately, and shares it as a file, to find out why something hasn't reached another device

## What's fixed

- With iCloud sync on, bookmarks, settings and history no longer wait to reach your other devices until the videos you just saved are in iCloud
- iCloud sync in Settings says how many saved files and changes are still going up to iCloud, and how far the saved file going up is, instead of showing them as synced, and tells you when iCloud didn't take a change
- With iCloud sync on, saved files and changes go up to iCloud and come in from it as soon as they can, instead of when iOS finds a good moment, which could take a minute for one video
- Sync now no longer locks the iCloud sync menu in Settings until everything has gone up
- Turning iCloud sync off and on again while it was sending no longer leaves it spinning in Settings
