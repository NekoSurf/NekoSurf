## What's new

### 🔒 More privacy settings
- Settings → Privacy can keep NSFW boards hidden until you tap the flame on Boards, and hide them again once you close the app
- While NSFW boards are hidden, their threads no longer show in your feed, search, bookmarks or history either
- Images on NSFW boards can stay covered until you tap them
- The Saved tab can be taken out of the tab bar; Saved then opens from History
- Saved files can be kept in the app, out of the Files app on iPhone or the Download folder on Android
- Saving to Photos can ask first
- The app switcher can show the app blurred on iPhone, or blank on Android, instead of what's open, with videos paused till you're back
- History can be kept only while the app is open: it's never stored or synced, and forgotten when you leave the app

### 🧹 Simpler storage settings
- One Clear cache button clears both images and videos
- Keep watched posts, Video cache limit, Clear watched posts and Clear recently visited are gone: clear your history on the History page
