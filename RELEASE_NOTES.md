## What's new

### 🖼️ Thread gallery
- New **gallery button** in every thread: see all of its images and videos as a grid
- Tap any tile to open it fullscreen and swipe through the rest

### 🔴 Live threads
- Open threads now update on their own; new posts appear at the bottom without moving what you're reading
- A **"new posts" pill** takes you straight to them
- Updates pause while the app is in the background and stop once a thread is archived or closed

### 🔔 New replies at a glance
- Bookmarks and **Continue** now show **"+N new"** for replies posted since you last read a thread

### 🎬 More video controls
- **Playback speed** in fullscreen: 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 2× or 0.5×
- **Auto-advance**: let fullscreen move on to the next item when a video ends, instead of looping (remembered between sessions)

### 🔍 Reverse image search
- New search button in the fullscreen viewer: look up any image on Google Lens, Yandex, SauceNAO or Bing
