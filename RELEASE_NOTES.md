## What's fixed

### 🎬 No more crash when leaving a video
- Leaving the app while a video plays in fullscreen could make it crash ("closed unexpectedly")
- Fullscreen videos now pause while the app is in the background and continue where they left off when you come back

### 🔔 Update notice on iPhone
- The "new version available" notice now waits until the update can actually be installed from TestFlight, instead of showing up a day or two early
