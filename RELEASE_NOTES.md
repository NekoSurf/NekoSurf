## What's new

### 🗄️ Thread archive
Browse a board's archived threads right in the app.
- New **archive button** on boards that keep an archive, next to the menu
- Newest first, with previews that load in as you scroll
- Grid and list view, following your board view setting
- Archived threads open like any other thread

### 🎬 Smoother videos
- Feed and fullscreen videos are much more stable: no more spinners that never go away, flickering back to the thumbnail, or crashes from too many videos loading at once
- Progress bars now show how much of a video has loaded
- Dragging the slider seeks once when you let go, instead of stuttering along the way
- Reopening a video you just watched starts instantly, without loading it again

### ↩️ Pick up where you left off
- **Auto-scroll to last seen** is now on by default
- After jumping, a toast lets you go **back to the top** with one tap

### ⚡ Faster everywhere
- Board search and sorting are instant, with no reloading
- Tabs keep their scroll position when you switch between them
- Threads with lots of images use far less memory
- Continue and Bookmarks check thread status with far fewer requests
- Quote links (>>123) open instantly

## Fixes
- Search now also finds words in the comment of threads that have a title
- Broken thumbnails show a placeholder instead of an error box
- "Last seen" now remembers the furthest post you read
