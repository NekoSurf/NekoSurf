Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

NekoSurf is designed around one goal: giving you the smoothest possible 4chan browsing experience while keeping your privacy first.

🌊
Liquid Glass Interface
The latest version introduces a full liquid-glass aesthetic — translucent panels, blur layers, and fluid transitions throughout the entire app. It feels as good as it looks.
  • Frosted glass cards and overlays
  • Smooth morphing transitions
  • Depth-aware blur layers
  • Consistent motion language across every screen
🔍
Smart Board Browsing
Navigate all 4chan boards instantly. Paste any 4chan link directly to jump straight into a thread.
  • Fast search across all boards
  • Paste a 4chan link → open the thread
  • Grid and list view toggle
  • Sort by bump order, newest, oldest, reply count, or image count
🎬
Media-First Experience
Images and videos are first-class citizens. Full WebM and MP4 support with a dedicated gallery viewer and swipe navigation.
  • WebM & MP4 playback
  • Swipeable full-screen gallery
  • Save to camera roll / gallery
  • File name and size context
  • Share saved media directly from the app
🔖
Bookmarks & Favorites
Keep track of the threads and boards you care about. NekoSurf shows you exactly when a bookmarked thread has been archived or deleted.
  • Favorite boards for home-screen quick access
  • Bookmark threads for later
  • Live archive / deleted status on bookmarks
  • Configurable retention controls
💬
Threaded Replies
Replies are truly threaded — each reply is nested under its parent post so conversations flow naturally and are easy to follow.
  • Full recursive reply threading
  • Auto-scroll to last-seen post
  • Inline media previews in threads
🔒
Privacy & Personalization
No account needed. No telemetry. NekoSurf keeps full control in your hands with per-feature toggles and local-only storage.
  • NSFW board visibility toggle
  • Watched-posts retention controls
  • Cache size display & one-tap cleanup
  • Dark mode
